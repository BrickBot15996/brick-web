At Asociația BrickBot we build robots with precision, and we treat your personal data with the same care. This policy explains, simply and transparently, what information we collect when you visit brickbot.ro, write to us, or support STEM education through a donation, and how we use it.

## 1. What data we collect and why

**Donations and sponsorships.** We collect only the data strictly needed to process the transaction and comply with financial law:

- Identification data: first name, last name and email address, to send you the confirmation and our thanks.
- Financial data: we do not store card details. Payments are processed securely by our payment partner, Stripe. We receive only the transaction confirmation and the history our accounting requires as an NGO.

**The contact form.** When you write to us through the form, we collect your name, email address, the topic you choose, the subject and your message.

- Your message is sent by email to contact@brickbot.ro, with a copy to the team department you selected. It is not saved in a database on our website.
- The emails stay in our team mailboxes only as long as we need them to answer you and follow up.
- To keep out spam, the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical signals from your browser to tell people from bots. It does not use advertising cookies.

**Visiting the site.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not build a profile of you; we see only aggregated numbers such as page views and the countries visits come from.

## 2. How we use your data

We do not sell or trade information. We use your data only to:

- process donations and sponsorships securely;
- issue the documents the law requires (receipts, sponsorship contracts);
- answer the messages you send us;
- communicate about your payment and, if you agreed to it, send short updates about the team's progress in robotics competitions.

## 3. Who else has access to your data

The only external services that handle your data are:

- **Stripe**, our payment processor.
- **Resend**, the email service that delivers contact form messages to our inbox.
- **Cloudflare**, which hosts this site and provides Turnstile and Web Analytics.
- **State authorities**, only when the law requires us to report the association's financial and accounting documents.

## 4. Your rights (GDPR)

You stay in control of the data you give us. You have the right to access it, correct it, or ask us to delete it (within the limits financial law places on keeping donation records). You also have the right to complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP).

## 5. Contact

If you have questions about how we protect your data, write to us at [contact@brickbot.ro](mailto:contact@brickbot.ro) or at 15 Cezar Bolliac Street, Focșani, Vrancea, Romania.
