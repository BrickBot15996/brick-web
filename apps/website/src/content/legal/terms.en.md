## 1. General information

This site is operated by Asociația BrickBot, a non-governmental, non-profit organisation headquartered at 15 Cezar Bolliac Street, Focșani, Vrancea, Romania, legally registered in Romania with tax ID (CIF) 50846996. The purpose of our platform is to support STEM education and to fund our robotics team's participation in national and international competitions.

## 2. Nature of transactions: donations and sponsorships

All payments made through this site are voluntary donations or sponsorships intended exclusively to support the educational activities of Asociația BrickBot.

- These contributions are not payment for commercial goods, services, pre-orders or financial investments.
- Donors do not receive material goods in exchange for the amounts given; their contribution is strictly philanthropic and supports the team's activity.
- Sponsorships are governed by a separate written contract between the sponsor and Asociația BrickBot.

## 3. Refund policy

Given the legal nature of donations to an NGO, transferred amounts are, in principle, non-refundable. We treat every interaction with transparency and fairness, however, and we will issue a full refund in these exceptional cases only:

- **Technical errors:** a donation was processed twice because of a system error.
- **Unauthorised transactions:** the payment was made fraudulently, without the cardholder's consent.

To request a refund for one of these reasons, contact us within 14 days of the transaction at [contact@brickbot.ro](mailto:contact@brickbot.ro). We review and resolve requests within 5–7 business days.

## 4. Payment security

All payments are processed in a secure environment by our partner, Stripe. Asociația BrickBot never asks for and never stores your card details. Payments are processed in RON or EUR, and the association charges no additional transaction fees.
